## GeM seller onboarding checklist (for AMClub's GeM-onboarding service)

1. **Choose seller type** — OEM (manufacturer), Authorized Reseller/Channel Partner, or Service Provider. A seller may represent only one OEM's brand per product category. *(org.gem.gov.in/sellerRegisterGuide)*
2. **Gather core identity/registration documents**: Aadhaar **or** Personal PAN (only one required); for companies, CIN; entity PAN; ITR details; active email; mobile number; bank account details; business/office address proof. GSTIN is **not mandatory**. *(gem.gov.in/userFaqs/seller)*
3. **If MSME**: obtain a UDYAM registration certificate, with the mobile number on it matching the registrant's — needed for MSE status, MSE purchase-preference eligibility, and several caution-money/ITR exemptions.
4. **If a startup**: obtain a DIPP/DPIIT recognition number linked to the registrant's mobile — mandatory for the startup registration path.
5. **Register the primary user account** on gem.gov.in using the documents above; create secondary user accounts afterward for staff who need bid-participation or order-management access.
6. **Pay caution money** (one-time), unless exempt: <₹1 Cr turnover → ₹5,000; ₹1–10 Cr → ₹10,000; >₹10 Cr → ₹25,000. **Exempt categories**: Artisans, Weavers, MSE SC/ST-owned, MSE Women-owned, ODOP sellers. Without payment, the seller cannot list new products/brands or join new bids. *(Caution: FAQ text references a historical "last payment date" of 30 Nov 2020 — confirm current applicability before advising MSMEs; see Not verified.)*
7. **If registering as OEM**: go to My Account → OEM Panel, select category & brand, upload brand-ownership/trademark documents (or a contract-manufacturing agreement if a non-manufacturing OEM), and submit the request. Approval TAT is ~15 days, granted per category — separate requests are needed for each category.
8. **If reselling**: obtain an OEM-issued Authorization Code for each OEM/brand before listing; check whether the category is "Q1" (OEM-only, no resellers) or "Q2" (OEM + authorized resellers) under CMS 2.0.
9. **Complete Vendor Assessment if required** for the category (mandatory for OEMs/resellers in select categories, run by RITES). Report is delivered within 15 working days of request; assessment disputes must be raised within 5 working days and are resolved by day 15–20. **Exemptions**: turnover >₹500 Cr, Central/State PSUs, direct-sale-only sellers, handicraft sellers, registered societies/trusts, BIS-licensed OEMs.
10. **List the catalogue**: pick the correct category, upload compliant images (≤300KB, jpg/png, white background, no watermark/logo, ideally 3 angles), supply BIS number where the category requires it, and set a price that meets the platform's mandatory minimum-discount-off-MRP rule. Expect a 96-hour approval turnaround; after publishing, only price/stock remain editable.
11. **Understand the fee stack that kicks in with volume**: once cumulative merchandise value crosses ₹20 lakh in a financial year, an Annual Milestone Charge of ₹10,000 + GST applies, plus slab-wise transaction charges on individual orders ≥₹10 lakh; a 2% TDS applies once cumulative charges exceed ₹30,000/FY; charges are refundable within 90 days for qualifying cancellations.
12. **Optional value-adds to plan for**: SAHAY (order/invoice-backed loans, proprietorships only) for working capital; Business Cockpit analytics subscription (₹6,00,000 + GST/year, aimed at larger OEMs) for category insights; the "GeM Sellers" mobile app for on-the-go order/payment/bid management.
13. **Ongoing compliance**: update registered particulars (address, bank, ownership, etc.) within 15 days of any change — non-compliance is grounds for suspension.

## Monetisation observed (GeM)

GeM's model is a hybrid, not a single take-rate: (1) **registration is free**; (2) a **one-time refundable-style caution-money deposit** tiered ₹5,000/₹10,000/₹25,000 by turnover, with broad exemptions for artisans, weavers, MSE SC/ST, MSE women, and ODOP sellers; (3) volume-triggered **transaction charges** — an Annual Milestone Charge of ₹10,000 + GST once a seller crosses ₹20 lakh GMV/year, plus slab-based charges on individual orders ≥₹10 lakh, with 2% TDS above ₹30,000 cumulative charges/FY; and (4) an optional premium **Business Cockpit** analytics subscription at ₹6,00,000 + GST/year aimed at large OEMs. No percentage "commission" figure is stated anywhere in the fetched pages — the model is entirely flat/threshold-based fees plus a refundable deposit, not a proportional take rate.

## Top 3 to adopt for AMClub

**1. Productize the GeM-onboarding checklist as an in-product wizard/checklist (source: GEM-onboarding checklist above)**
- *User problem*: MSME buyers of AMClub's "GeM onboarding" service don't know which documents, fees, and steps apply to them, causing scope confusion and back-and-forth before an order can even start.
- *Metric moved*: RFQ→quote ≥70% in 48h (clearer scope means providers can quote faster and more confidently); dispute rate <5% (fewer "you didn't tell me I needed X document" disputes).
- *Effort*: **S** — this is a content/checklist feature (a structured intake form + reference checklist), not new infrastructure; the source facts are already gathered above.
- *Principle check*: Escrow kept — yes, unaffected. No off-platform leakage — yes, the checklist lives inside the RFQ flow, doesn't push users to gem.gov.in directly for the transaction itself. No negotiation/auction — yes, purely informational.
- *Main risk*: GeM rules change over time (fee thresholds, exempt categories, caution-money enforcement) — the checklist must be date-stamped and periodically re-verified against official pages, or it will misinform MSMEs.

**2. Add a lightweight provider-facing analytics dashboard (views / RFQ-inbox conversion / response time), inspired by JustDial Business's "views, leads, and engagement" analytics (JD-4)**
- *User problem*: Providers currently have no visibility into why they're winning or losing RFQs (is it price, response time, profile completeness?), which hurts retention and quote quality over time.
- *Metric moved*: 90-day repeat ≥25% (providers who understand their funnel stay active longer); RFQ→quote ≥70% in 48h (providers self-correct slow response times).
- *Effort*: **S–M** — largely a reporting layer on data AMClub already captures (RFQ inbox, quote win/loss, response time), no new data collection needed.
- *Principle check*: Escrow kept — yes, unaffected. No off-platform leakage — yes. No negotiation/auction — yes, purely observational/analytics, not a bidding mechanic.
- *Main risk*: If exposed carelessly, providers could reverse-engineer AMC Score (currently deliberately hidden from buyers per the brief) — the dashboard must be designed to avoid leaking ranking logic while still being useful.

**3. Stage-wise quality/delivery evidence capture (photos + short inspection notes) at order milestones, inspired by Zetwerk's "photos and inspection reports at every stage" (ZW-3)**
- *User problem*: Buyers (especially first-time MSME buyers) have no visibility into work quality until final delivery, which drives disputes and erodes trust, particularly for goods-adjacent or physical-deliverable services (e.g., licensing document packages, physical inspections for GeM/factory-licence work).

- *Effort*: **M** — needs a milestone-attached photo/note upload UI plus storage, which likely already exists partially in AMClub's "order timeline/milestones, doc exchange" feature — mostly an extension, not new infrastructure.
- *Principle check*: Escrow kept — yes, and this can strengthen escrow release logic (require evidence before milestone payout). No off-platform leakage — yes. No negotiation/auction — yes.
- *Main risk*: Added documentation burden may slow down small/simple orders where it's unnecessary overhead; should be opt-in or auto-triggered only for higher-value/physical-deliverable categories, not applied uniformly.
