   - Metric: search→checkout; 90-day repeat.
   - Effort: S (from order events). Principles: kept.
   - Risk: small-n. Show only above N orders.
2. **Spec enrichment in the RFQ pre-check.** Suggest the implied standards and documents: for services, "which documents you'll need"; for goods, "DIN/IS standard, MTC" (AB-04).
   - Metric: RFQ→quote.
   - Effort: M. Principles: kept (the buyer confirms).
   - Risk: over-specification scaring off small providers.
3. **"Get quotes for what you browsed"** one-tap RFQ (AB-06).
   - Metric: search→checkout.
   - Effort: S. Principles: kept.
   - Risk: low-intent RFQs. Keep the pre-check.
4. **Samples plus customisation level on AMC Mart listings** (AB-14).
   - Metric: search→checkout (goods).
   - Effort: M. Principles: kept (the sample is paid via escrow).
5. **Delay compensation as part of escrow** (AB-17): an automatic small credit when a provider misses the delivery date.
   - Metric: review ≥4.2, dispute <5%.
   - Effort: M. Principles: kept.

   - Effort: M.

### 7. AI horizon (Alibaba-inspired)

**1. Agent that compares shortlisted providers before any RFQ exists.**
- **What unlocks it:** cheap reasoning over provider records.
- **Foundations to lay now:**
  - Normalised provider facts table (credentials, capacity, on-time %, reorder %) with a source and a timestamp per field.
  - A "shortlist" event.
- **Dark version now:** a compare tray for up to 4 providers, rule-based.
- **Excluded list:** no.

**2. Credibility cross-check against outside records.** Alibaba uses customs; for us: GST return-filing status, MCA, ICAI/Bar Council.
- **What unlocks it:** dependable browser agents and document understanding.
- **Foundations to lay now:**
  - Store the raw evidence per check.
  - Store each provider's declared "main category" so it can be compared with observed order categories.
- **Dark version now:** flag providers whose paid orders fall mostly outside their declared category.
- **Excluded list:** no.

**3. Negotiation agent.**
- **Excluded list:** yes, it touches "no per-buyer negotiation". Record only.

**4. Metered AI credits for buyers.**
