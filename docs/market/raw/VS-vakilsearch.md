## FINAL REPORT — Vakilsearch/Zolvit, 23 Sep 2026, not logged in

### 1. Coverage
- **Visited:** home, GST registration page (packages, FAQ anchors), lead modal, talk-to-a-lawyer.
- **Not reached, and why:**
  - Checkout: a lead form, not a cart.
  - Customer dashboard / compliance app: login.
  - Order tracking: login.
- **Checkpoints posted:** 1–2.

### 2. Journeys
- **B1:** mega-menu or search → service page → package → "Get Started" → lead form (email, mobile, city) → a sales follow-up (inferred) → payment later.
- **B5:** money-back "until we file".
- **B6:** a 12-month filing bundle inside the Elite tier.

### 3. Monetisation
- Fixed-fee packages from ₹399 to ₹3,499 for GST.
- Per-minute consults (₹2.80/min; ₹99 intro).
- Compliance subscriptions (inferred).
- International incorporation.

### 4. Trust
- **Reviewer CA on each page.** Scope: the named CA reviewed the page content. Prominence: medium.
- **Google/Trustpilot ratings.** Scope: third-party review counts. Prominence: high.
- **ISO 27001.** Scope: company certification. Prominence: medium.
- **Money-back guarantee.** Scope: refund until the application is filed. Prominence: high.

### 5. AI
- The "AI-Driven" hero search behaved as keyword autocomplete in my test: irrelevant state pages, and the intent was ignored.

### 6. Top adopt (Vakilsearch-specific)
1. **"Choose this if…" one-liners and a recommended tier on packages** (VS-01).
   - Metric: search→checkout.
   - Effort: S. Principles: kept.
2. **Registration + 12-month compliance bundles** (VS-01 Elite). Sold as a package with milestone payouts from escrow.
   - Metric: 90-day repeat ≥25%, take rate.
   - Effort: M. Principles: kept (escrow per milestone).
   - Risk: long-duration escrow and provider churn.
3. **Needs advisor:** "I run a fabrication unit in Kurnool → you likely need Udyam, GST, factory licence, pollution NOC (CTE/CTO), trade licence". Routes to categories and says it isn't advice.
   - Metric: search→checkout.
   - Effort: M. Principles: kept (routing, not legal advice).
   - Risk: must not look like legal advice.
4. **Company-name availability checker + free calculators** as the top of the funnel (VS-11).
   - Effort: S–M.
5. **Honest govt-dependency disclaimer on every govt-facing package** (VS-04). It pairs with our timer pause.
6. **Content reviewed by a verified professional, with a date** (VS-03).

### 7. AI horizon
**1. Business-profile → obligations engine.** Udyam + GSTIN + sector + state → a list of registrations/licences and their renewal dates.
- **What unlocks it:** reliable reasoning over state-specific rules plus cheap document understanding of certificates.
- **Foundations to lay now:**
  - A structured "licence held" table per buyer: type, number, issue date, expiry, issuing authority, uploaded certificate.
  - A curated rule table for AP and Telangana first, with sources.
  - An eval set of 50 business profiles with their correct obligation lists, reviewed by a CA.
- **Dark version now:** from Udyam sector + state, show "businesses like yours also bought: …", based on orders.
- **Excluded list:** no. Label it LATER (compliance calendar).

**2. Voice consult triage.** A caller describes the problem in Telugu → structured brief → routed to a verified CA/lawyer package. No AI advice.
- **Foundations to lay now:** "problem type" taxonomy; consented voice-to-brief pairs.
- **Excluded list:** no, as long as it is voice routing and not video.

### 8. Anti-patterns
- Perpetual "50% off" / "75% OFF Today!" anchors.
- Price shown, but "Get Started" is a lead form with WhatsApp opt-in on by default.
- The click on Standard upsells to the Premium promise ("GSTIN in 24 hours").
- Contradictory timelines on one page.
- The "AI-driven" label on keyword autocomplete.

### 9. Open
