## 1. Headline findings

1. **The transaction model is validated.** Every lead directory leaks the deal off-platform:
   - IndiaMART: payment by NEFT to the seller; "protection" covers non-delivery only, claimed within 60 days.
   - TradeIndia: "View Number" on every card.
   - Vakilsearch: a price shown, then a lead form.
   - Only Fiverr, Alibaba (Trade Assurance) and Xometry hold the money the way we do. Escrow, contact masking, structured quotes and verified-purchase reviews are real differentiators. Keep them, and say them louder (§4).
2. **Competitors publish measured trust. We compute it and hide it.**
   - They show repeat-purchase % (Alibaba, 1688), on-time/fulfilment % (Alibaba, 1688), response rate and calls answered (IndiaMART), and queue depth (Fiverr).
   - We compute on-time, responsiveness, confirmations and disputes in the AMC Score, but buyers see only response time.
   - Our **"Top Rated" badge is dead code**: it renders, and search sorts by it, but nothing ever sets it. This is decision **D1**.
3. **RFQ completeness is where incumbents invest.**
   - IndiaMART: a live 0–100 strength meter while you type, plus a "Fill using AI" chat.
   - Alibaba: a one-line → full-spec generator that adds implied standards (DIN 933, test reports).
   - Ours: a voice-first intake with a quality pre-check after submit. That's good, but there's no guidance while typing and no spec enrichment.
4. **Package merchandising is thin.**
   - No tiers, no "choose this if…", no add-ons, no bundles.
   - Vakilsearch's registration + 12-month-filing bundle and Fiverr's priced add-ons are the retention and order-value levers we lack.
5. **We had our own drip-pricing bug, fixed in this PR.**
   - The package price note said "Price shown is what you pay. GST invoice included."
   - Checkout adds 18 % GST on top: a ₹6,999 package charges ₹8,258.82.
   - Competitors add fees at checkout (Fiverr ~7.4 %, Moglix ₹19); ours went further and said the price was final. The note now reads "Plus 18% GST at checkout".
6. **Competitor AI is mostly shallow.** Two exceptions:
   - Alibaba **AI Mode**: an agentic sourcing assistant, credit-metered, which includes AI *negotiation*. That part is NOT-NOW for us.
   - Xometry's **instant quote from CAD**.
   - Elsewhere: TradeIndia's AI search takes ~30 s and ignores MOQ; Vakilsearch's "AI-driven" search is keyword autocomplete.
   - Alibaba AI Mode is English/USD only. **Indic voice is a real edge**, but ours is switched off.
7. **Monetisation is moving to buyers.** IndiaMART now sells buyers a "Verified Business Buyer" tier (₹999/month to ₹9,999/year): a procurement assistant, a badge, and "no spam calls". Alibaba sells AI Mode Pro.
   - Our counter-position: commission on success.
   - The rule to write down: **trust is earned, never sold** (§5).

## 4. Where AMClub is already ahead (keep; say it louder)

- **Escrow covers quality**, not just non-delivery. IndiaMART's protection is non-delivery only, NEFT only, with a 15–60 day claim.
- **Structured quotes + side-by-side compare, capped at 7, with contact masking.** IndiaMART hands the buyer's number to sellers, and "no spam calls" is a paid tier.
- **Fixed-scope packages with delivery days and Buy Now.** Competitors show indicative prices, or a price that opens a lead form.
- **Commission on success**, not pay-per-lead. IndiaMART's cost per lead is ₹37–55; TradeIndia's seller packages run ₹49k–₹18.4L.
- **In-order disputes with both parties' statements**, versus generic complaint forms.
- **Reviews from verified purchases only.**
- **Indic voice RFQ**, when switched on. Alibaba AI Mode is English/USD only.
- **Trust we don't sell.** IndiaMART's TrustSEAL (which payment protection requires) is bundled into a ₹60k/yr ad plan, and its STAR/LEADING labels are paid ranks.

## 5. Anti-patterns to avoid (and one of ours)

| Pattern | Seen at | AMClub rule |
|---|---|---|
| Fees or tax revealed only at checkout | Fiverr, Moglix, **AMClub (fixed here)** | Show GST and any fee before checkout |
| Trust or rank sold as a badge | IndiaMART TrustSEAL / STAR / LEADING, Alibaba "Top sponsor" | **Trust is earned, never sold.** If featured placement arrives (LATER), it is labelled "Sponsored" and never looks like a quality badge |
| Contact handed over by default; pre-ticked sharing or opt-ins | IndiaMART, TradeIndia "View Number", Alibaba business card, Vakilsearch WhatsApp toggle | No pre-order chat; nothing pre-ticked |
| Fake urgency: countdowns, permanent "50–75 % off" | TradeIndia, Vakilsearch, Moglix, Alibaba | Discounts are real and time-bounded, or not shown |
| An "AI" label on something that isn't AI; slow AI behind a spinner | Vakilsearch, TradeIndia | Label AI only where a model acts; give a text answer fast or fall back |
| AI dropping the buyer's quantity or MOQ | Alibaba RFQ generator, TradeIndia AI search | Quantity/unit are required, typed fields; add an eval case for it |
| Contradictory timelines on one page | Vakilsearch | One delivery-days field per package |
| A dead badge | **AMClub "Top Rated"** | Wire it or remove it (D1) |

## 6. AI landscape and the foundations to lay now

### 6.1 What competitors ship
| Portal | AI feature | What it really does |
|---|---|---|
| Alibaba | AI Mode | Agentic sourcing: design, trends, vetting, comparison, logistics, **negotiation**. Credit-metered; English/USD only |
| Alibaba | AI RFQ generator | One line → full spec with implied standards; the buyer applies, regenerates or discards. Missed the quantity field |
| Alibaba | AI supplier vetting | Cross-checks a supplier's claims against customs records |
| IndiaMART | Fill using AI | Text chat → restatement + 1 question with answer chips → fills the RFQ form |
| IndiaMART | Procurement Assistant (paid) | Need → RFQ → curated suppliers → quotes with a "% match" |
| IndiaMART | Voice search | 9 Indic languages |
| Xometry | Instant Quoting Engine | Geometry + config → 3 price/lead-time options, with no human in the loop |
| Fiverr | AI gig summary | Bulleted summary of the gig and its reviews |
| TradeIndia | AI search | ~30 s; right on location, ignores MOQ; no explanation |
| Vakilsearch | "AI-driven" search | Keyword autocomplete |

### 6.2 Foundations: what exists and what to add
The horizon assumption: within about 12 months, frontier-quality reasoning at today's small-model prices, near-free Indic speech, and reliable vision and browser agents. The pattern for each item: **capture the data now; run a "shadow" version that is logged and shown to nobody; ship only when its measured error is acceptable.**

**F1. Quote outcome labels**
- **Enables:** provider fit %, price guidance, the procurement agent's shortlist.
- **Exists:** `quotes.decline_reason` enum; quote statuses; paid orders.
- **Add now:**
  - A label for quotes that lose to another quote (the winner's price and time deltas).
  - Log which compare flags the buyer viewed.
  - An eval set of (RFQ, quotes, chosen) triples.

**F2. Typed buyer constraints**
- **Enables:** matching, fit %.
- **Exists:** `rfqs.budget_min/max_paise`, `needed_by`.
- **Add now:** must-haves as a typed list (credential, language, on-site).

**F3. Typed spec ontologies**
- **Enables:** a CAD → price band (guidance only), matching, facets.
- **Exists:** the STEP/DXF summary in `rfq_intake_extractions.proposed`.
- **Add now:**
  - Save CAD features (bbox, holes, entity counts) as typed fields on the RFQ.
  - A manufacturing spec schema: process, material, tolerance, finish, inspection.
  - Goods attributes per category.
  - **Package deliverables as line items**, not prose.

**F4. Provider facts + verification evidence**
- **Enables:** the public checklist, a credibility cross-check agent, the "declared vs actual" category flag.
- **Exists:** `provider_verifications` (kind, status).
- **Add now:**
  - Per check: source, method (API or manual), fetched-at, and an evidence hash.
  - A nightly GSTIN-status re-check.
  - Declared main category vs categories of paid orders.
  - Write `last_seen_at`.

**F5. Search telemetry**
- **Enables:** conversational search with a reasoned shortlist; search evals.
- **Exists:** PostHog events.
- **Add now:** parsed constraints per query; result click → order attribution; relevance thumbs; a golden set.

**F6. Consented multimodal corpora**
- **Enables:** Indic voice search, photo/drawing → RFQ, HSN from an image.
- **Exists:** voice RFQ and document intake pipelines.
- **Add now:**
  - Audio → transcript → edited-text triples, with consent.
  - Image → final-RFQ pairs with per-field corrections.
  - A Hindi/Telugu service-term → category synonym table.

**F7. Obligations data**
- **Enables:** the "needs advisor" now, a compliance calendar LATER.
- **Exists:** none.
- **Add now:**
  - A "licences held" table per buyer: type, number, issued, expiry, authority, certificate.
  - A rule table for AP/Telangana with sources.
  - 50 business profiles with CA-reviewed obligation lists as an eval set.

**F8. Goods order attributes**
- **Enables:** reorder reminders, cluster demand forecasting, freight bands.
- **Exists:** Mart order lines.
- **Add now:** SKU attributes, pincode/weight/volume on goods orders, and reorder intervals.

**F9. Per-user AI cost**
- **Enables:** metered AI inside membership (LATER).
- **Exists (Have):** `ai_invocations.user_id`, `feature`, `task_class`.
- **Add now:** nothing.

**F10. A standard shadow-prediction table**
- **Enables:** safe rollout of the price band, fit %, photo extraction and review summaries.
- **Exists:** the eval harness and `ai_invocations`.
- **Add now:** one `shadow_predictions` (feature, subject, predicted, actual, error) table reused by every shadow model.

**Excluded despite being seen:** AI negotiation (Alibaba), reverse-auction RFQs (Alibaba sellers, GeM), "request a discount from several suppliers" (Amazon Business, in a pending file). All are NOT-NOW (§8.3). Record them; don't build them.

## 7. Decisions only the founder can make

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Show measured trust stats to buyers? ADR-010 says buyers never see a number | **Yes, but only individual measured stats** (on-time %, repeat-buyer %, response rate, orders completed), above sample thresholds. **Never the composite score.** Wire "Top Rated" to a score band or remove it.
